# Firewood

The woodshed is the first workshop: a workplace that takes one good and gives another. The player places it with the `Woodshed` tool, `K`, whose button reads Shed, on 2 by 2 tiles of open, level ground, and the settlers build it from 6 logs in 20 seconds of work at 1×. When it is finished, the settler without a job nearest it takes its one place and becomes its woodcutter, and the `Settlement news` log names it. The woodcutter walks to the unit of logs on a stockpile tile nearest the shed within 30 tiles of it, carries 1 log to the shed's door, and splits it there in 10 seconds of work, and 2 firewood go into the shed's store, which holds 40. While the households sleep at the camp, the settlers without a job carry firewood from the shed to the camp, which holds 12. The Stockpiles panel at the top left counts the firewood in woodsheds and at the camp in its `Firewood in stores` row. The shed's panel says what it does, names its woodcutter, and reads `Places`, `Firewood: n of 40`, and `Doing`, what its woodcutter does. The woodcutter's panel reads `Job: Woodcutter` and what it does: `Walking to a stockpile for a log`, `Carrying a log to the woodshed`, `Splitting a log`, and, idle, `Idle, no log in a stockpile within 30 tiles of the woodshed`, `Idle, the woodshed is full`, or `Idle, the building sites need the logs`. This scenario plays a woodshed on seed 7 with five chopped trees, follows one log from the stockpile to the firewood, and runs on until every log is split. `src/sim/workshops.test.ts` proves the rules, the full shed, and the logs kept for the sites, and `src/ui/selection.test.ts` and `src/ui/news.test.ts` prove the words.

## Sub-features

- `firewood-tool` picks the woodshed tool with K, its button reading Shed, and places a woodshed site with a click.
- `firewood-site` labels the site `Woodshed site`, with `Logs n/6`, no stone, and its work.
- `firewood-panel` reads the finished shed's title, its note, its woodcutter by name, `Places 1 of 1`, its firewood, and what the woodcutter does, and counts 1 settler with a job.
- `firewood-news` names the settler who became the woodcutter.
- `firewood-job` reads `Job Woodcutter` in the woodcutter's panel.
- `firewood-split` reads the woodcutter's panel through one log: to the stockpile, carrying 1 log to the shed, and splitting it at the door, and the `Firewood in stores` total, and the shed's and the camp's firewood together, rise by 2, or by a further 2 for each later log split.
- `firewood-stop` reads `Idle, no log in a stockpile within 30 tiles of the woodshed` in the shed's panel once every log is split.
- `firewood-layout` keeps the toolbar of 10 tools, the Stockpiles panel with its firewood row, and the shed's panel apart and inside the page at 960 by 540.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the game at `?seed=7`. The forest's edge lies a few tiles east of the camp.
- Press C and click five trees at the forest's edge east of the camp, for the shed's 6 logs and 4 to split.
- Press P and drag over a few tiles of open ground between the camp and the forest's edge.
- Press K and click open ground at the forest's edge east of the camp. Press Escape.
- Run at 8×. The label over the site goes at about noon, and the news names the woodcutter.
- Click the shed to read its panel. Click the woodcutter and run at 1× to watch it fetch a log, carry it to the shed, and split it.
- Run on at 8×. In the late afternoon the shed's panel says the woodcutter has no log left to split.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `$V doctor` passes, or you use `$V run firewood`, which runs doctor itself.
- One page opens at 960 by 540 on `?seed=7` with the settlers, no `food` setting, recorded, and `clock: 'paused'`, a few game minutes after 08:00.

- **Take the view.** `takeForageView` takes the foraging clip's view, 23 m looking east from the camp to the forest's edge. `a new game opens with no woodshed firewood and all 8 settlers without a job` reads the Stockpiles panel.
- **Give the orders.** It presses C and clicks each of `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.trees`, the tiles (137, 129), (138, 130), (134, 131), (135, 134), and (139, 130). `a Chop click on each of the 5 trees marks one tree` reads the status line after each. It presses P and drags `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.stockpile`, from (131, 131) to (132, 132), and `a Stockpile drag adds the 4 tiles in front of the camp` reads `4 tiles join the stockpile zone`. It presses K and clicks `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.shed`, the tile (136, 127). `K picks the Woodshed tool, whose button reads Shed, which places a woodshed site with a click` reads the pressed button, its face, the hint, the preview line, and `Woodshed site placed.` `the woodshed site's label reads 0 of 6 logs, no stone, and waits for materials` reads the label by its role and name, and it takes `firewood-site.png`.
- **Read the shed.** It runs at 8× until the label is gone and pauses. `the woodshed is finished on day 1 between 11:00 and 13:30` reads the clock when the label went. It clicks `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.roof`. `at ... the woodshed's panel says what it does, names its woodcutter with its one place filled, and reads its firewood and what the woodcutter does` reads `Woodshed`, the note, one name in `Woodcutter`, `Places 1 of 1`, `Firewood n of 40`, and a woodcutter's `Doing`, and it takes `firewood-shed-panel.png`. `the Stockpiles panel counts 1 settler with a job and 7 with none, and the news names the woodcutter` reads the top left and `<name> became a woodcutter.` in the news.
- **Read the woodcutter.** It clicks the fullest cells of tunic pixels until a panel reads `Job Woodcutter`, passing over the cells beside a click that opened another settler, and when none does it runs 3 game minutes and tries again, `FIREWOOD_SEEK.tries` times at most. `at ... the woodcutter's panel reads its job and what a woodcutter does` needs the shed's woodcutter, and it takes `firewood-woodcutter-panel.png`.
- **Follow one log.** It runs until the woodcutter's `Doing` reads `Walking to a stockpile for a log`. Then it runs at 1× while the woodcutter walks or carries and at 2× while it splits, and pauses whenever the `Doing` changes. At each stop it takes `firewood-follow-<n>.png`, reads the woodcutter's panel and the `Firewood in stores` total, and finds the selection ring. It stops at the first stop after a `Splitting a log` stop. `<name>'s panel follows one log at paused stops: walking to a stockpile for it, carrying it to the woodshed, and splitting it, in that order` lists every stop. `the woodcutter carries 1 log at a time, and nothing on its way to the stockpile or while it splits` reads `Carries`. `the clip finds <name>'s selection ring at every paused stop` needs the ring at each. `<name> picks the log up at the stockpile and splits it at the woodshed's door` measures the ring at the first carrying stop from `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.stockpileMiddle` and at the first splitting stop from `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.door`, the tile (135, 128), within `FIREWOOD_AT`. Then it clicks the camp and the shed and reads both panels, and while they and the total do not hold 2 more firewood than the `Firewood in stores` total at the last splitting stop, or a further 2 for each later log split, it runs `reach` 5 game minutes on and reads them again, until 19:05. A settler without a job carries each unit from the shed to the camp, and a unit in hands is in neither store, so one stop can read short. A unit of an earlier batch in hands can leave the total at the last splitting stop odd, so the count starts from that total rounded up to whole batches. Nothing else takes firewood out of either store on day 1, and the camp's 12 hold all 8, so once the last carrier is in they hold 2 for each log split, and the stops cannot miss it. `when the split is done, the shed's and the camp's panels together, and the Firewood in stores total, read 2 more firewood, or a further 2 for each later log split` needs that, and its detail lists every stop and the one that reached it.
- **Watch it stop.** It runs at 8× with the shed selected until its `Doing` reads `Idle, no log in a stockpile within 30 tiles of the woodshed`, and pauses. `at ..., with no log left on the stockpile, the shed's panel says its woodcutter stopped for lack of logs, and the shed and the camp come to hold every log beyond the shed's 6 split into 8 firewood` reads the shed's panel and `Logs 0`, then clicks the camp and the shed at stops 5 game minutes apart until the shed's `Firewood` and the camp's sum to 8 with `Firewood in stores 8`, or until 19:05, for the same reason. It takes `firewood-shed-stopped.png`. `at 960x540 the woodshed's panel keeps clear of every other panel on the page` compares its box with those of the clock, the toolbar, the Stockpiles panel, the camera controls, and the status line and the news when they show, and `at 960x540 the woodshed's panel shows every one of its rows` compares its height with its rows'.
- **Fit the panels.** It presses P, so the status line shows. `the toolbar holds its 10 tools, the Woodshed among them, each inside the page and clear of the others and of the clock, at 960x540` reads the names and the boxes of the toolbar's buttons. `the Stockpiles panel, with its Firewood in stores row, keeps clear of the clock, the status line, the toolbar, and the camera hint at 960x540` compares the boxes as `hauling` does. It takes `firewood-960x540.png`.
- **The clip.** `firewood-split.gif` runs from the first follow stop to the stop after the split, cropped by `FIREWOOD_CLIP` to the clock, the Stockpiles panel, the woodcutter's panel, the camp, the stockpile, and the shed, with 32 colors, and made with `decimate`: every frame that repeats the one before is dropped and the rest play at 8 a second, so a paused stop passes in one frame and the running parts play at the pace SwiftShader drew them, several times the game's own. The clock in each frame says its game time.
- **Proof.** Run `$V run firewood`. The evidence folder holds the clip, the screenshots, and `result.json` with every check, including `no page errors`.

## Gotchas

- `FIREWOOD_SPOTS` are where the camera rig and the picker put each tile's center at `FORAGE_VIEW`, from `scripts/aim.ts` with the command in the comment above `FIREWOOD_SEARCH`. A change to the camera's opening view, its input rates, or the map of seed 7 moves them.
- The woodcutter takes logs only from stockpile tiles, never from the ground, so the scenario marks a stockpile. The felled logs the site does not take are carried there by the settlers without a job, and the panel read `Logs 4` when the shed stood.
- The five trees give 10 logs: 6 build the shed and 4 become 8 firewood. A tree more or less changes the 8 the stop check needs, and with more trees the stop comes nearer the dusk.
- The page gives its orders a few game minutes after 08:00, not at the first tick as `scripts/firewood-year.ts` does, so the woodcutter can be another settler. On rj-firebat Merek became the woodcutter at 11:54 in one run and Elsi at 12:12 in the next. The scenario reads the name from the panel.
- When the shed is finished its builders stand at its corners, and a woodcutter that starts from a far corner walks behind the shed, out of sight from this view, before it comes out on the way to the stockpile. On rj-firebat one stayed hidden from 12:06 to 12:21, and a run that tried 5 frames to 12:21 failed to select it. The search tries `FIREWOOD_SEEK.tries` more frames 3 game minutes apart, and on each frame it passes over the cells beside a click that opened another settler, whose tunic fills several cells of 12 pixels, so it clicks each settler in sight once. A woodcutter found late is followed from its next walk to the stockpile.
- The door the woodcutter splits at is the one nearest the stockpile tile it took the log from, (135, 128) for every tile of this stockpile, on the camera's side of the shed.
- At the dusk of day 1, 19:12, the woodcutter walks to the camp and its panel and the shed's say so. In `src/sim` runs with the orders 5 to 90 ticks late the woodcutter stopped for lack of logs at 17:23 to 18:07, and on rj-firebat the page paused on it at 17:05 and at 17:30. A stop not reached by `FIREWOOD_UNTIL`, 19:05, fails the scenario.
- On rj-firebat, with the game of f51e00d, `firewood-split.gif` took 2.1 MB for 14 s, inside the 4 MB a clip of the stage's demo set may take, and a run of the scenario took about 2 minutes.
- While the households sleep at the camp, the settlers without a job carry the split firewood from the shed to the camp, so the shed's panel reads less than was split. On a pylon with 6 CPUs, with the game of 9d30a73, the shed held 2 and the camp 6 when the woodcutter stopped at 17:41, and `Firewood in stores` read 8. A unit in a carrier's hands is in neither store. On the game of a0d841f a settler picked a unit up within the pause after the split, and the shed read 1 and the camp 0 at 13:51, so the scenario reads the stores at short stops after the split until both carriers have walked to the shed and on to the camp. In `src/sim` runs with the orders at 08:13 to 08:18 both units were in a store again 26 to 79 game minutes after the split, and the woodcutter's next split came 4 to 8 game minutes after the slowest, so a stop after that split reads 2 more and fails. `FIREWOOD_SPOTS.camp` are the points around the camp's fire at `FORAGE_VIEW`, from `scripts/aim.ts` with the tile (127, 127), which the scenario clicks until one selects the camp.
- The page never fills the shed or keeps its logs for a site. `src/sim/workshops.test.ts` and `src/ui/selection.test.ts` cover `Idle, the woodshed is full` and `Idle, the building sites need the logs`.
