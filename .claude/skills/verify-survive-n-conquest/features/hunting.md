# Hunting

Deer are the second source of food. They graze and wander on grass and forest at 0.8 tiles a second, with short pauses, and run at 3 tiles a second for 4 seconds from any settler within 6 tiles. A deer is not selectable. The player places a hunter's lodge with the `Hunter's lodge` tool, `L`, whose button reads Lodge, on 2 by 2 tiles of open, level ground, and the settlers build it from 8 logs and 4 stone in 30 seconds of work at 1×. When it is finished, the two settlers without a job nearest it take its places and become hunters, and the `Settlement news` log names them. A hunter stalks the nearest deer within 40 tiles of the lodge that no other hunter follows, stops 10 tiles from it or nearer, aims for 4 seconds, and shoots, and 2 shots in 3 kill. A miss makes the deer run. A kill leaves 6 food on the ground, and the hunter carries them 1 unit at a time to the nearest store with room, the camp or a finished barn, and the food in barns and at the camp in the Stockpiles panel rises. The lodge's panel says what it does, names its hunters, and sets its filled places, as the hut's does. A hunter's panel reads `Job Hunter` and what it does: `Stalking a deer`, `Aiming at a deer`, `Walking to a kill to pick up food`, `Carrying food to a barn` or `to the camp`, and, idle by day, `Looking for deer`, `Idle, no deer within 40 tiles of the lodge`, or `Idle, waiting for room in a store`. This scenario plays a barn and a lodge on seed 7 through day 2, watches a deer run from a settler that morning, and follows one hunt from the stalk to the food in the barn. `src/sim/animals.test.ts`, `src/sim/hunting.test.ts`, and `src/sim/firstYear.test.ts` prove the rules, the misses, the dusk, and the year, and `src/ui/selection.test.ts` proves the idle words.

## Sub-features

- `hunting-deer` shows deer that wander the meadow at a walk.
- `hunting-flight` shows a deer that runs from a settler near it, faster than a deer walks.
- `hunting-unselectable` leaves no panel after a click on a deer.
- `hunting-tool` picks the lodge tool with L, says it places a lodge on 2 by 2 tiles, and places a lodge site with a click.
- `hunting-site` labels the site `Hunter's lodge site`, with `Logs n/8`, `Stone n/4`, and its work.
- `hunting-panel` reads the finished lodge's title, its note, its two hunters by name, and `Places 2 of 2`, and counts 2 settlers with a job.
- `hunting-news` names the two settlers who became hunters.
- `hunting-hunt` reads a hunter's panel through a hunt: stalking, aiming, walking to the kill, and carrying 1 food to a barn.
- `hunting-food` raises the barn's food and the `Barn and camp food` total by the kill's food.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the game at `?seed=7`. The rock lies north of the camp, the north meadow beyond it, and the forest's edge east of both.
- Press C and drag over the trees east of the rock. Press M and drag over the rock's eastern edge. Press B and click open ground between the rock and the forest for the barn.
- Run at 8× to the morning of day 2. A deer that walks toward the settler at the quarry turns and runs north.
- When the barn's label is gone, press L and click open ground at the meadow's southern edge, north-west of the barn. Press Escape.
- Run at 8×. The lodge's label goes in the early or mid afternoon, and the news names the two new hunters.
- Click the lodge to read its panel, and click a hunter to read what it does. Run at 2× and follow it: it walks toward a deer, stops, aims, and shoots, then walks to the kill and carries the food to the barn 1 unit at a time.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `$V doctor` passes, or you use `$V run hunting`, which runs doctor itself.
- One page opens at 960 by 540 on `?seed=7` with the settlers, no `food` setting, recorded, and `clock: 'paused'`, a few game minutes after 08:00.

- **Give the orders.** The page takes `HUNT_ORDER_VIEW`, 42 m from the south over the rock, the barn's tiles, and the forest's edge, drags Chop over `HUNT_ORDERS.chop`, the tiles (141, 100) to (150, 112), and Quarry over `HUNT_ORDERS.quarry`, (124, 105) to (125, 108), and clicks the barn on (134, 108) with B. `the Chop and Quarry drags mark trees and rock, and the barn site is placed` reads the status line after each.
- **Watch a deer run.** It takes `HUNT_CLOSER`, 23 m looking east over the meadow, where north is to the left, and stops at each of `FLIGHT_STOPS`, every 2 game minutes from 05:40 to 06:20 on day 2. At each stop it takes `hunting-flight-<time>.png` and finds the deer by the pixels of `DEER_COAT` and the settlers by `MORNING_TUNIC`. `a deer runs from a settler near it faster than a deer walks` needs a chain of at least 3 steps between stops, each faster than a walking deer could go and in about the same direction, that starts within `FLIGHT.settlerPx` pixels of a settler and ends farther from it. It counts a move of `HUNT_TILE_PX` pixels as at most a tile, and gives a walking deer 0.8 tiles a second for one game minute more than the clock read, so the bound on the walk is generous. `a click on a deer selects nothing` clicks the middle of the coat of the deer that ran, where it stands at the last stop, away from every settler.
- **Place the lodge.** It runs at 8× until the barn's label is gone, pauses, presses L, and clicks `HUNT_SPOTS.lodge`, the tile (129, 100). `once the barn stands, ... L picks the Hunter's lodge tool, which places a lodge site with a click` reads the pressed button, the hint, the preview line, and `Hunter's lodge site placed.` `the lodge site's label reads 0 of 8 logs, 0 of 4 stone, and waits for materials` reads the label by its role and name.
- **Read the lodge.** It runs at 8× until the lodge's label is gone and pauses. `the lodge is finished on day 2 between 06:00 and 18:00` reads the clock when the label went. It clicks the lodge's roof. `at ... the lodge's panel says what it does and names its two hunters, with both places filled` reads `Hunter's lodge`, the note, two names in `Hunters`, and `Places 2 of 2`, and it takes `hunting-lodge-panel.png`. `the Stockpiles panel counts 2 settlers with a job, and the news names the two who became hunters` reads the top left and the news log. It reads the barn's panel and the `Barn and camp food` total.
- **Follow a hunt.** It turns and pans to `HUNT_VIEW`, 23 m looking west over the lodge to the meadow and the lake, and clicks the fullest patches of tunic until a panel reads `Job Hunter`. `at ... a hunter's panel reads its job and what a hunter does` needs one of the lodge's hunters. Then it runs at 2× while the hunter stalks, aims, or looks, and at 4× while it walks to the kill or carries food, and pauses whenever the hunter's `Doing` changes and at least every `HUNT_LOOK_EVERY` game minutes. At each stop it takes `hunting-follow-<n>.png` and finds the selection ring. While the hunter stalks or aims it also finds the deer nearest the ring within `HUNT_FRAME_DEER` pixels, and frames the point between them. It pans that point back to `HUNT_AIM_AT` when it lies more than `HUNT_RECENTRE` pixels away. It stops at the first stop after a `Carrying food to ...` stop whose `Doing` differs, when the unit is in the store. `<name>'s panel reads the hunt at paused stops: stalking, aiming, walking to the kill, and carrying its food to a barn, in that order` lists every stop, and `the hunter carries the food 1 unit at a time` reads `Carries 1 food` at every carrying stop.
- **Read the barn.** It pans back to `HUNT_VIEW` and clicks the barn's roof. `the food of the kill rises in the barn's panel and in the Barn and camp food total` compares both with the readings at the lodge's finish. No hut stands, so every unit that enters a store on day 2 comes from a kill. It takes `hunting-barn-after.png`.
- **The clips.** `hunting-deer-runs.gif` runs from the 05:48 stop to the 06:18 stop, cropped by `FLIGHT_CLIP` to the clock, the meadow, and the quarry. `hunting-hunt.gif` runs from the first stop that reads `Stalking a deer` to the stop after the delivery, cropped by `HUNT_CLIP` to the clock, the Stockpiles panel, the hunter's panel, and the land around the hunter, up to the toolbar. Both are made with `decimate`: every frame that repeats the one before is dropped and the rest play at 8 a second, so a paused stop passes in one frame and the running parts play at the pace SwiftShader drew them, several times the game's own. `hunting-hunt.gif` also keeps 2 of every 3 frames left, with `speed: 1.5`, to stay under the 4 MB a clip of the stage's demo set may take. The clock in each frame says its game time.
- **Proof.** Run `$V run hunting`. The evidence folder holds the clips, the screenshots, and `result.json` with every check, including `no page errors`.

## Gotchas

- `HUNT_ORDERS` and `HUNT_SPOTS` are where the camera rig and the picker put each tile's center at the three views, from `scripts/aim.ts` with the commands in the comment above `HUNT_SEARCH`. A change to the camera's opening view, its input rates, or the map of seed 7 moves them.
- The orders go in from the south, because from the west the rock hides the quarry tiles on its eastern edge, and the picker takes the rock in front of them.
- A pan of more than about 600 pixels about the middle of the page starts its drag on the toolbar and moves nothing. The scenario drags in pieces of at most 250 pixels.
- The page follows the first hunter its clicks find, so the lodge stands where both hunters' kills are likely to lie nearer the barn than the camp. In `src/sim` runs with the orders 5 to 90 ticks late and the lodge 2 or 8 ticks after the barn, a lodge on (131, 105) gave 16 hunters of whom 4 carried their first unit to the camp, after a deer south or east of the barn, and 1 carried none before dusk. A lodge on (129, 100), at the meadow's southern edge, gave 15 of 16 whose first unit went into the barn before the dusk at 19:12, and 1 whose went to the camp. A hunter of that one kind fails the barn check.
- In `src/sim` terms deer 11 of seed 7 starts to run at 05:55 to 05:57 on day 2 from (128.6, 100.1), 6.1 tiles from the settler who quarries at (126.5, 105.5), and stops at about (132, 89) by 06:16, for every delay of the orders from 5 to 90 ticks. On the page the deer read 501, 239 at 05:56 and 236, 219 at 06:14.
- `DEER_COAT` also matches the trunks of the two pink trees and the logs at the barn site. Those spots stand still, so no chain of fast steps starts on them.
- A tile of the meadow is 14 to 33 pixels across at `HUNT_CLOSER`, narrow where it lies far from the camera. The flight's tiles are lower bounds, and the settler's distance is in pixels, 220 of them about 7 to 10 tiles there.
- The lodge stood at 14:13 and at 14:40 on rj-firebat, after the barn at 06:47 to 06:48. In the scripted runs it stood between 12:06 and 16:30, and a lodge placed a few ticks later can stand 3 hours later, because it waits for logs and stone that the barn's builders carry.
- A hunter may miss. After a miss its panel reads `Looking for deer` for a moment and `Stalking a deer` again, usually after the same deer. The check needs the phases in order, not one aim.
- Settlers without a job carry the kill's food too, and so does the other hunter's kill. The barn can rise by more than the followed hunter's unit.
- The dusk of day 2 falls at 19:12. A hunt that has not delivered its first unit by `HUNT_UNTIL`, 19:05, fails the scenario, because at dusk the hunter puts the unit down where it stands.
- The page never reaches the second dawn after a kill, when food left on the ground is gone, nor a winter. `src/sim/hunting.test.ts` covers the dusk, and deer stay through the winter.
