# Clothing

The tailor's hut is the second workshop. A deer a hunter kills leaves 1 hide on the tile beside its 6 food, and a settler without a job carries the hide to the nearest stockpile tile with room, never into the camp, a barn, or a house. The Stockpiles panel at the top left counts the hides on stockpile tiles in its `Hides` row. The player places the hut with the `Tailor's hut` tool, `T`, whose button reads Tailor, on 2 by 2 tiles of open, level ground, and the settlers build it from 8 logs in 30 seconds of work at 1×. When it is finished, the settler without a job nearest it takes its one place and becomes its tailor, and the `Settlement news` log names it. The tailor walks to the hide on a stockpile tile nearest the hut within 30 tiles of it, carries it to the hut's door, and does that again, and with 2 hides in the hut it works them for 60 seconds at the door, and 1 piece of clothing goes into the hut's store, which holds 8. The hut's panel says what it does, names its tailor, and reads `Places`, `Clothing: n of 8`, and `Doing`, what its tailor does. The tailor's panel reads `Job: Tailor` and what it does: `Walking to a stockpile for a hide`, `Carrying a hide to the tailor's hut`, `Making clothing`, and, idle, `Idle, no hide in a stockpile within 30 tiles of the tailor's hut` or `Idle, the tailor's hut is full`. A settler without clothing then fetches a piece from the hut and wears it, which `wearing` covers. This scenario plays a lodge and a tailor's hut on seed 7, follows a hide from a kill to the stockpile, follows the tailor with the hut's panel open until it reads 1 piece, and reads the hut's panel once the tailor has stopped for lack of hides. `src/sim/clothing.test.ts` proves the rules, the full hut, the dusk, and the hungry tailor, and `src/ui/selection.test.ts` and `src/ui/news.test.ts` prove the words.

## Sub-features

- `clothing-hides` shows a `Hides` row at the top left that reads 0 in a new game and goes up by 1 when a hide is put down on a stockpile.
- `clothing-tool` picks the tailor's hut tool with T, its button reading Tailor, and places a tailor's hut site with a click.
- `clothing-site` labels the site `Tailor's hut site`, with `Logs n/8`, no stone, and its work.
- `clothing-panel` reads the finished hut's title, its note, its tailor by name, `Places 1 of 1`, `Clothing 0 of 8`, and its tailor idle for lack of hides, and counts 1 settler with a job.
- `clothing-news` names the settler who became the tailor.
- `clothing-job` reads `Job Tailor` in the tailor's panel.
- `clothing-kill` follows a hunter through its last aim to the kill, and picks up the kill's food.
- `clothing-hide` follows a settler without a job from the camp to a kill's hide and back to the stockpile with `Carries 1 hide`.
- `clothing-piece` reads the hut's panel through two hides carried in and the work, until it reads `Clothing 1 of 8`.
- `clothing-stop` reads `Idle, no hide in a stockpile within 30 tiles of the tailor's hut` in the hut's panel after its first piece.
- `clothing-layout` keeps the toolbar of 10 tools, the Stockpiles panel with its Hides row, and the hut's panel apart and inside the page at 960 by 540.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the game at `?seed=7`.
- Press C and click the 8 trees nearest the camp, at the forest's edge east of it. Press M and click the rock tile north west of the camp.
- Press P and drag over 3 by 3 tiles of open ground on the camp's west side. Press L and click open ground beside it for the lodge. Press T and click open ground north of the camp for the tailor's hut. Press Escape.
- Run at 8×. The hut stands in the afternoon of day 1, and the news names the tailor. The lodge stands on the morning of day 2.
- Click the hut to read its panel. Run on, and watch the Hides row at the top left once the hunters have made a kill.
- Keep the hut's panel open and run on into day 3, until it reads 1 of 8 clothing.

## Gotchas

- `CLOTHING_SPOTS` are where the camera rig and the picker put each tile's center at `CLOTHING_VIEW`, from `scripts/aim.ts` with the command in the comment above `CLOTHING_SEARCH`. A change to the camera's opening view, its input rates, or the map of seed 7 moves them.
- The orders go in at 08:30 with the clock paused, but `reach` stops within 2 game minutes of its target, and a minute moves the whole hunt: in `src/sim` runs with the orders at 08:30, 08:31, and 08:32 the first kill fell at 08:30, 09:59, and 09:38 on day 2, by different hunters and at different places. The scenario reads every name from a panel and follows whichever hunter and carrier it finds.
- The 8 trees give the 16 logs the lodge and the hut need, and the rock tile 8 stone for the lodge's 4. With no logs left over, the settlers without a job have little to carry once both stand, and one of them sets out for a hide within a few game hours of its kill. On rj-firebat the search found Jorrin on his way to a hide at 11:22 on day 2, after a kill at 09:12.
- Hunters on seed 7 kill in the forest around the camp, and the tree crowns hide the hunter's ring and the hide. The hide's follow starts from the camp, where the settlers without a job wait on open ground, not from the kill.
- The hides of two kills can lie out at once, so the carrier the search finds may carry the other hunter's hide.
- The tailor takes a hide as soon as one lies on a stockpile, so the Hides row reads 1 only until the tailor picks it up. The follow pauses when the carrier's `Doing` changes, while the hide still lies there.
- The second hide came at 18:30 on day 2 on rj-firebat, and the tailor worked the batch from then until the dusk at 19:12 and on the morning of day 3. The work done before the dusk stays in the hut, so the piece came at about 09:00 on day 3.
- The tailor's hut's panel is the tallest of the panels, 267 pixels with its note on four lines, and at 960 by 540 it starts at y 217, 1 pixel below the Stockpiles panel's bottom edge at 216. One more row at the top left would make them overlap.
- On rj-firebat, with the game of this branch, a run of the scenario took about 20 minutes.
