# Cold

Winter is days 10 to 12, and each of its 3 nights is cold. A settler's night is judged when it comes home at dusk, at its meal. It is warm with two of three sources of warmth: it sleeps in a house, a fire burns where it sleeps, and it wears clothing. In this scenario nobody has clothing, because no tailor's hut stands, so only a house with a fire is warm. `wearing` plays a winter dusk at the camp with clothed settlers. A house's fire takes 2 firewood when its household comes home on a winter night, and the camp's fire takes 4 when the first settler comes home to the camp. A house holds 6 firewood and the camp 12. A household with a house carries firewood home from the nearest woodshed, and the settlers without a job carry it to the camp while a household sleeps there. A settler without a warm night has a cold night, works at half the rate after it, and freezes at its third cold night in a row. The Stockpiles panel shows `Firewood in stores` and `Firewood in homes`. A house's panel reads `Firewood n of 6`, the camp's `Firewood n of 12`, and a settler's `Cold nights`: `None`, or `n, cold`. The `Settlement news` log says `Day d, HH:MM. A and B had a cold night.` and `... froze.` A flame stands on the roof of each house whose fire burns tonight. The camp's fire shows on the map on every night, as it did before, so only the camp panel's `Firewood` row tells whether it took its firewood. This scenario plays two houses and a woodshed on seed 7 through the whole winter: the two households in houses stay warm, and the two at the camp freeze in the last night. `src/sim/cold.test.ts` proves the rules, `src/ui/selection.test.ts` and `src/ui/news.test.ts` the words, and `bun scripts/winter-year.ts two-houses nothing` prints the first winter of the same rules from the first tick.

## Sub-features

- `cold-orders` gives every order from the toolbar at 08:15 on day 1: a Chop drag, a Quarry drag, a Stockpile drag, a woodshed, and two houses.
- `cold-homes` reads both house panels once the houses stand: households 1 and 2 live in them, so the other 4 settlers sleep at the camp.
- `cold-stocked` reads both houses full at 6 firewood before the first winter dusk, `Firewood in homes` as their sum, and `Firewood in stores` as the shed's firewood and the camp's.
- `cold-fires` reads each house 2 firewood lower, `Firewood in homes` 4 lower, and the camp 4 lower after the first winter dusk, and finds the flame over each roof.
- `cold-news` reads that the 4 settlers at the camp had a cold night on days 10 and 11 and froze on day 12, and that nobody in a house did.
- `cold-panel` selects a settler of the camp with a click after the first winter night and reads `Cold nights 1, cold`.
- `cold-living` counts 4 living settlers at the top left after the last winter dusk, and reads both households in the house panels with nobody dead.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the game at `?seed=7&food=200`. The `food` setting fills the camp so that nobody starves before winter.
- Zoom out a little. Press C and drag over the forest's edge east of the camp, press M and drag over the two rock tiles at the foot of the outcrop north of the camp, and press P and drag over a few tiles between the camp and the forest.
- Press K and click the ground just north of the camp. Press H and click two spots west of the camp, one above the other.
- Run at 8×. The houses stand on day 2 or 3, and their households carry firewood home once the woodcutter has split some.
- Zoom in on the houses and the camp. Run to about 17:00 on day 10, then watch the dusk at 1×. A flame shows on each roof as its household comes home, and the news names the settlers at the camp.
- Click a settler of the camp the next morning to read its cold nights. Run to the dusk of day 12 and read who froze.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `$V doctor` passes, or you use `$V run cold`, which runs doctor itself.
- One page opens at 960 by 540 on `?seed=7&food=200` with the settlers, recorded, and `clock: 'paused'`, a few game minutes after 08:00.

- **Give the orders.** `takeForageView` takes `COLD_ORDER_VIEW`, 61 m above the point between the camp and the houses, and `reach` stops at 08:15. It presses C, M, and P and drags each pair of `COLD_ORDERS`, then presses K and H and clicks the shed and each house. `at day 1 08:15 the orders mark 35 trees, 2 rock tiles, and 9 stockpile tiles, and place a woodshed site and two house sites` reads the status line after each order, the site labels, and the clock, which has not moved. It takes `cold-orders.png`, wheels `COLD_CLOSER` in to the near view of 27 m at the same point, drags `COLD_NEAR_PAN` to move the near view's look-at point to (127, 123), and takes `cold-view.png`.
- **Read the homes.** At 12:00 on day 4 it takes `cold-built.png` and clicks each house in `COLD_SPOTS.houses`, then the camp. `at ... both houses are finished and households 1 and 2 have moved in, one in each` needs no site label and each house's `Household` row to name its two members. `the house panels name 4 of the 8 settlers of seed 7, so the other 4 sleep at the camp` reads the names against `HUNGER_NAMES`.
- **Before the dusk.** At 17:00 on day 10 it takes `cold-before-dusk.png` with nothing selected and reads the Stockpiles panel and the panels of both houses, the camp, and the shed. `at ..., before the first winter dusk, each house holds 6 firewood and Firewood in homes is the sum of the two house panels` and `at ... Firewood in stores is the shed's firewood and the camp's, and the camp holds at least the 4 its fire burns` read them.
- **Watch the dusk.** It runs at 1× to 18:20, reading the news every 250 ms, and `reach` stops at 18:30. It takes `cold-after-dusk.png` and reads the same panels. `at ..., after the first winter dusk, each house's fire has burnt 2 firewood and Firewood in homes reads 4 less` and `at ... the camp's fire has burnt 4 firewood` compare them with the stop before. `at ... a flame stands over the roof of each house, and none did at ...` counts `FLAME` pixels in each house's `sign` box in both screenshots, at most `COLD_FLAME.most` before and at least `COLD_FLAME.least` after. `the news of the first winter dusk names the 4 settlers at the camp as having had a cold night, and none of the 4 in houses` reads every text the day 10 line took.
- **The clip.** `cold-winter-dusk.gif` runs from the stop at 17:00 to the stop at 18:30 on day 10, cropped by `COLD_CLIP` to the clock, the Stockpiles panel, the houses, the camp, and the news, with 48 colors and `decimate`: every frame that repeats the one before is dropped and the rest play at 8 a second, so a paused stop passes in one frame and the 1× run plays at the pace SwiftShader drew it, several times the game's own. The clock in each frame says its game time.
- **A cold settler.** At 08:00, 09:00, and 10:00 on day 11 it clicks the fullest cells of tunic pixels, passing over the interface and each house's `body` box, until a panel names a settler that sleeps at the camp. `at ... a settler of the camp, selected by a click after the first winter night, reads 1 cold night and cold` needs `Home None, sleeps at the camp`, `Cold nights 1, cold`, and a name of the camp's 4. It takes `cold-settler-panel.png`.
- **The second dusk.** It stops at 17:00 on day 11, runs at 1× to 18:20 reading the news, and stops at 18:30. `the news of the second winter dusk names the same 4 settlers at the camp, and none in houses` reads the day 11 line.
- **The last dusk.** The same at day 12. `at the dusk of day 12, their third cold night in a row, the news says the 4 settlers at the camp froze, and nobody in a house` needs the 4 in the `froze` line and no `had a cold night` line on day 12. It takes `cold-last-dusk.png`, and `at ... the Stockpiles panel counts 4 living settlers, and the house panels name their households with nobody dead` reads the top left and both house panels.
- **Proof.** Run `$V run cold`. The evidence folder holds the clip, the screenshots, and `result.json` with every check, including `no page errors`.

## Gotchas

- The near view moved west by `COLD_NEAR_PAN` when the winter readiness panel came, because the panel covers the page from x 441 to 792 and y 108 to 249 at 960 by 540 from the first day of autumn, and the first house's flame lay under it: at the dusk of day 10 its sign box read 0 flame pixels against the 40 needed, and the second house's 201. `wearing` keeps the view before the pan, and its own spots.
- Every pixel in `COLD_ORDERS` and `COLD_SPOTS` comes from `scripts/aim.ts`, with the commands in the comment above `COLD_SEARCH`. The near view is the order view and a further wheel toward the same look-at point, so aim.ts reaches it as one view with the summed wheel and that point as its `target`. A change to the camera's opening view, its input rates, or the map of seed 7 moves them.
- The orders land at 08:15 to 08:17, whatever minute the page first paused at. In `src/sim` runs of the layout with the orders at 08:13 to 08:19, the woodshed stood by 13:36 on day 1, the second house between 16:10 on day 2 and 15:31 on day 3, both houses held 6 firewood and the camp 12 at 17:00 on day 10, and the camp's 4 froze between 17:25 and 17:54 on day 12. Which settler becomes the woodcutter changes with the minute, so no check names it. The households and their houses do not change: the lowest household gets the first finished house.
- A line of the news takes a new text each time a settler joins it, `Jorrin had a cold night.`, then `Jorrin and Fenna had a cold night.`, and so on, and a line shows for 30 s of wall time. The scenario reads the news while the clock runs and counts the distinct names of every text a day's line took.
- In the winter morning light the thatch of the houses reads as tunic yellow. A run that clicked the fullest cells anywhere clicked the houses 16 times at 08:00 on day 11 and found a settler of the camp only at 10:00. With each house's `body` box passed over, the next run selected Dorran with its first click at 08:00.
- The 35 trees give 70 logs: 30 build the shed and the houses, and the rest are split until the shed holds its 40, so the shed reads `Idle, the woodshed is full` at the dusk of day 10.
- On a pylon with 6 CPUs a game day at 8× took about 44 s of wall time, and a `run` of the scenario 15 minutes, most of it the wait at 8× from day 4 to day 10. In the run that passed, `cold-winter-dusk.gif` took 2.5 MB for 145 frames, inside the 4 MB a clip of the stage's demo set may take, and the flame boxes read 0 before the dusk and 141 and 172 after it.
